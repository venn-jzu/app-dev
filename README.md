## About the Movie

**Cars** is an animated *sports comedy* film produced by **Pixar Animation Studios**. It follows a hotshot rookie race car who learns that friendship matters more than winning.

[^1]: Cars was released in theaters on June 9, 2006.

## Plot Summary

Lightning McQueen is on his way to the final race of the Piston Cup when he gets stranded in the small town of Radiator Springs. There he learns that winning isn't everything.

## Terms Used in the Movie

Piston Cup
: The championship racing trophy that Lightning McQueen wants to win.

Radiator Springs
: A forgotten town along Route 66.
